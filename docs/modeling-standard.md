# Modeling standard

Applies to procedural geometry in `src/sim/parts/` and to CadQuery scripts in `cad/scripts/`.

- Units: procedural scenes in meters. CadQuery scripts in inches, exported to meters (scale 0.0254) in the GLB.
- Origin and axes for an assembly: origin at the flow-path center on the assembly centerline. Flow along +X. Stem or vertical along +Z in CadQuery (rotated to +Y in the viewer); +Y up in procedural scenes.
- Node names equal record IDs. A part of an assembly is `{ASSEMBLY-ID}-{PART}`. The build check (scripts/integrity.mjs) fails the build when a record claims a node that does not exist or a GLB contains a node no record claims.
- Generic geometry only: proportions from public catalog dimensions or from the dimension sheet in `cad/dims/`. No brand features, part numbers, or proprietary internals.
- Budgets: per-assembly GLB under 2 MB compressed; per-scene resident triangles under 500,000 on the phone variant and 1.5 million on the laptop variant.
- Materials: use the shared `MAT` set in `src/sim/parts/primitives.jsx` for procedural parts; the CadQuery exporter maps part roles to the same palette.
- Motion: two-position valves travel over a fixed time; mechanisms use simple kinematics and carry the "schematic motion" label.
- Section: the section plane through the stem axis and the bore (world z = 0 in the viewer) must show the interior correctly, so bores, cavities, and seats are real solids, not painted on.
- Labels: sprite labels only (no DOM labels inside the canvas).
- Framing: a component page opens framed on its own part with the rest of the assembly translucent; F toggles between the part and the whole assembly. Tall stacks (the WL pressure control stack) therefore read at part level, not assembly level.
- Own vocabulary: tab names, status names, viewer keys, and page names are this project's own and are not to be aligned with any other site's wording.
- Size variants: an assembly that exists in several bores and ratings is exported once per size as `{ID}.{size}.glb` (sizes 4-10K, 4-15K, 5-10K, 5-15K, 7-10K, 7-15K). The record's `glb` is the default file and `variants` lists the rest; the build check binds every record node in every variant. Node names never change between variants.
- Flange envelopes come from `cad/dims/WH/flanges.json` (public 6BX table, E2). Body, bonnet, and actuator proportions are the project's own.
- Photographs: field and yard photographs are read for overall proportion only (block height to flange diameter, actuator length to bore, stack height to well count). No logo, paint scheme, nameplate, or distinctive design feature is reproduced, and no photograph enters the repository. Photographs stay in `reference-photos/` outside the repo.
- Context nodes: a part drawn only to give context (the casing around a downhole tool) is listed in the record's `ghost_nodes` and the viewer draws it translucent and leaves it out of framing.
- Standard frac stack: tree adapter, lower master (manual), upper master (hydraulic), cross with a manual then a hydraulic wing valve each side, crown valve (hydraulic), flanged inlet block, swab valve (hydraulic), top adapter. The procedural simulator tree and the CadQuery tree share this configuration and the same node names.
- Assemblies by system: WH (gate valves, frac stack, zipper, wellhead, flow iron, tree saver), WL (pressure control stack), CT (pressure control stack and injector), DT (plug on setting tool), FB (choke manifold). Each keeps its node names in its own `cad/scripts/{SYSTEM}/` script; the build check page lists every file with its triangle count and budget status.
- Vertical zipper: the treating line from the missile enters the skid through an inlet isolation valve (`WH-ZIPPER-INLETVALVE`) into a low header; each well has a vertical leg (`WH-ZIPPER-LEG`: tee, riser, spool) carrying a lower isolation valve (`WH-ZIPPER-ISOVALVE`) and an upper working valve (`WH-ZIPPER-VALVE`) with a top elbow and flanged outlet (`WH-ZIPPER-OUTLET`) toward the tree; actuators point away from the trees. The procedural zipper and the CadQuery zipper share these names; the simulator binds well 1 to the store valves `iso`, `zipIso`, and `zipWork`.
- Missile: low-pressure side is two suction headers along the deck edges with an outlet per pump (`PP-MISSILE-LPHEADER`, `PP-MISSILE-LPOUTLET`); high-pressure side is the discharge header of junction fittings down the centerline (`PP-MISSILE-HPHEADER`) with a check valve and swivel-arm stub per feed port (`PP-MISSILE-CHECKVALVE`, `PP-MISSILE-SWIVELARM`), a transducer, the relief valve with its vent line (`PP-MISSILE-PRV`), and the outlet flange (`PP-MISSILE-OUTLET`). Pumps park nose-in on both sides at one pitch per fitting; the simulator lengthens the missile with the pump count computed from the fleet and the rating.
- Sliding sleeve path: the tree carries a ball launcher (`WH-FRACTREE-BALLLAUNCHER`, scene only) in place of the top adapter; the downhole scene draws sleeve subs (`DT-FRACSLEEVE`, `DT-TOESLEEVE`) with port windows, inner sleeve, seat, and ball, and openhole packers (`DT-OPENHOLEPACKER`) when that system is chosen. The cut-away model `DT-FRACSLEEVE.glb` shows the sleeve shifted open with the ball seated and the casing joints as ghost context.
- Pad Setup and basins: terrain is procedural (value noise relief flattened across the pad, vertex-colored ground, instanced vegetation by type and density, sky and fog colors) from the basin table in `src/sim/store.js`. Basin values are typical public ranges rounded for training and loaded as editable starting values; they are not operator or well data.
- Fleet power: `PP-POWERGEN` draws the unit for the fleet chosen (turbine generators and switchgear, reciprocating gensets, grid substation, gas conditioning skid, fuel trailer); electric pumps replace the engine and transmission with a motor and VFD cabinet (`PP-FRACPUMP-MOTOR`).
- Downhole realism: formation drawn as nine layers with bedding planes and a few natural fractures on the section face; perforations as six tunnels at 60 degree phasing with crushed zones and casing holes; fractures as a main plane with two branches and a stress-shadow taper across a stage; plugs with slips, cones, element, and ball; coiled tubing with a motor BHA; openhole systems draw a rugose borehole wall instead of cement.
- Artificial lift: the production tree takes a `lift` prop; `PumpingUnit` (animated crank, beam, bridle, and polished rod), `EspSurface`, `GasLiftSurface`, and `PlungerController` draw beside or on it, and `ProductionString` in the downhole scene draws the tubing tail with the pump, ESP, mandrels, or plunger. CAD: `AL-BEAMUNIT.glb` (15 nodes, mid-upstroke) and `AL-RODPUMP.glb` (cut-away, tubing as ghost). Sand control: `SC-GRAVELPACK.glb` (cut-away; casing, formation, and washpipe as ghosts).
- Well colors: `WELL_COLORS` in `src/sim/parts/surface.jsx` tints valve bodies on each well's tree and its flanged run from the zipper leg, in well order, so the same color reads at the tree and at the leg; partner wells are drawn in a darkened tint.
- Cut-away models: a script that removes half of a model to show the interior removes the -Y half in CadQuery coordinates, because the viewer rotates Z-up to Y-up and the default camera looks from +Z, which is CadQuery -Y; the cut face then faces the camera on open. The viewer's own section plane (S) is for models that are not pre-cut.
- Training events: `EVENTS` in `src/sim/store.js` lists the injectable faults with the phases they apply to; each has a recovery sequence in `nextSteps` and its own state in the `events` slice (misfire armed, valve fault, sand timer). Random mode draws from the events valid for the phase at a low rate per second.
- Production hookup: the `hookup` slice steps rig, tubing, tree, done; the scene draws `BopStack` and `WorkoverRig` (block travels while tubing runs) until the tree step, then the production tree, and the lift equipment only when done. CAD: `RG-BOPSTACK.glb` cut-away (annular, double rams, spool, adapter, bell nipple; tubing head as ghost).
- Scoring: `DEDUCT` in `src/sim/store.js` holds the weights (interlock 2, valve against the sequence 3, overpressure 8, kickout 10, screenout 12); `EVENT_TARGETS` holds the time-to-recover targets per injected event (1 point per 15 s over, max 8). Alarm transitions in the tick deduct only when the operator caused them (an injected relief valve lift or screenout does not); a valve command is "against the sequence" when it moves a valve out of a state the current `nextSteps` list has already checked off. `scoreOf` grades A 90, B 80, C 70, D 60. The score is a training weight over a schematic model and is labeled as such.
- Guided lessons: `src/sim/lessons.js` lists eight lessons, each with `setup` and `pad` overrides applied to the defaults, an optional `prep(get, set)` that puts the job at the starting point (snapping valves and finishing stages), and ordered `steps` with a `done(s)` predicate, a hint, and an optional valve or action for the button. Only the first unfinished step is evaluated each tick and a met step stays met. A lesson that stages a fault (kickout in L3, screenout in L4) lists the code in `exempt` so it costs nothing; the lesson score adds a time penalty of 1 point per 10 percent over `targetSec` (max 20). Results persist in localStorage only (`src/sim/progress.js`).
- Session summary: `src/sim/SessionSummary.jsx` reads the live job or the `lastJob` snapshot taken on reset; `@media print` in `src/index.css` hides everything but `.print-area` on white. The JSON export is a Blob download; there is no server.
- Pad asset detail (Drop 11): `src/sim/parts/primitives.jsx` carries the shared chassis and fittings: `Trailer` (gooseneck end set by `front`, tri-axle duals, fenders, landing gear, deck grating, rub rails, bumper, lights; deck top at `DECK_Y` = 1.18 m), `Axle`, `Wheel` (dual option), `Handrail`, `Ladder`, `Stair`, `Hose` (sagging tube), `UnionNut` (three-lug hammer union, used by `Pipe`), and `Studs` (one instanced mesh per flange). Surface assets in `surface.jsx` build on these; every asset keeps its record-bound group and part names (`PP-FRACPUMP-n-ENGINE`, `-MOTOR`, `-VFD`, `-TRANSMISSION`, `-POWEREND`, `-FLUIDEND`, blender `-TUB`, `-AUGER`, `-DISCHARGEPUMP`, `-SUCTIONPUMP`, and so on) so hover picking and the build check are unchanged. Proportions come from the pad photographs (engine-to-fluid-end split, hood height, tank length to width, silo height to diameter); no livery, logo, or distinctive body shape is reproduced. Painted surfaces use the paint materials (`paintWhite`, `paintRed`, `cream`, `chassis`) rather than bare `steel`, which renders dark without an environment map; the rendering pass is a separate drop.
- Draw-call budget: repeated detail is merged into one BufferGeometry per material with `Merged` (`primitives.jsx`), keeping indexed geometry; a gate valve is five meshes (body, steel parts, stem, handwheel or actuator, lamp) instead of sixteen; a tree or a zipper leg draws every stud in one `StudField` instanced mesh; cylinders take their segment count from their radius (`segFor`). Small parts (studs, rails, ladders, hoses, union nuts, rims) do not cast shadows. Reference at the default 4-well pad: about 2,700 draw calls per frame including the shadow pass and 420k triangles; the Drop 10 pad was 3,450 calls and 340k triangles. `window.__padworksStats` and `window.__padworksScene` expose the counters and the scene for the headless checks (`scripts/probe-perf.mjs`).
