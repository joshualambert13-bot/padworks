# Modeling standard

Applies to procedural geometry in `src/sim/parts/` and to CadQuery scripts in `cad/scripts/`.

- Units: procedural scenes in meters. CadQuery scripts in inches, exported to meters (scale 0.0254) in the GLB.
- Origin and axes for an assembly: origin at the flow-path center on the assembly centerline. Flow along +X. Stem or vertical along +Z in CadQuery (rotated to +Y in the viewer); +Y up in procedural scenes.
- Node names equal record IDs. A part of an assembly is `{ASSEMBLY-ID}-{PART}`. The build check (scripts/integrity.mjs) fails the build when a record claims a node that does not exist or a GLB contains a node no record claims.
- Generic geometry only: proportions from public catalog dimensions or from the dimension sheet in `cad/dims/`. No brand features, part numbers, or proprietary internals.
- Budgets: per-assembly GLB under 2 MB compressed; per-scene resident triangles under 500,000 on the phone variant and 1.5 million on the laptop variant.
- Materials: use the shared `MAT` set in `src/sim/parts/primitives.jsx` for procedural parts; the CadQuery exporter maps part roles to the same palette.
- Motion: two-position valves travel over a fixed time; mechanisms use simple kinematics and carry the "schematic motion" label.
- Section: the section plane through the stem axis and the bore (world z = 0 in the viewer) must show the interior correctly, so bores, cavities, and seats are real solids, not painted on.
- Labels: sprite labels only (no DOM labels inside the canvas).
- Framing: a component page opens framed on its own part with the rest of the assembly translucent; F toggles between the part and the whole assembly. Tall stacks (the WL pressure control stack) therefore read at part level, not assembly level.
- Own vocabulary: tab names, status names, viewer keys, and page names are this project's own and are not to be aligned with any other site's wording.
- Size variants: an assembly that exists in several bores and ratings is exported once per size as `{ID}.{size}.glb` (sizes 4-10K, 4-15K, 5-10K, 5-15K, 7-10K, 7-15K). The record's `glb` is the default file and `variants` lists the rest; the build check binds every record node in every variant. Node names never change between variants.
- Flange envelopes come from `cad/dims/WH/flanges.json` (public 6BX table, E2). Body, bonnet, and actuator proportions are the project's own.
- Photographs: field and yard photographs are read for overall proportion only (block height to flange diameter, actuator length to bore, stack height to well count). No logo, paint scheme, nameplate, or distinctive design feature is reproduced, and no photograph enters the repository. Photographs stay in `reference-photos/` outside the repo.
- Context nodes: a part drawn only to give context (the casing around a downhole tool) is listed in the record's `ghost_nodes` and the viewer draws it translucent and leaves it out of framing.
- Standard frac stack: tree adapter, lower master (manual), upper master (hydraulic), cross with a manual then a hydraulic wing valve each side, crown valve (hydraulic), flanged inlet block, swab valve (hydraulic), top adapter. The procedural simulator tree and the CadQuery tree share this configuration and the same node names.
- Assemblies by system: WH (gate valves, frac stack, zipper, wellhead, flow iron, tree saver), WL (pressure control stack), CT (pressure control stack and injector), DT (plug on setting tool), FB (choke manifold). Each keeps its node names in its own `cad/scripts/{SYSTEM}/` script; the build check page lists every file with its triangle count and budget status.
- Vertical zipper: the treating line from the missile enters the skid through an inlet isolation valve (`WH-ZIPPER-INLETVALVE`) into a low header; each well has a vertical leg (`WH-ZIPPER-LEG`: tee, riser, spool) carrying a lower isolation valve (`WH-ZIPPER-ISOVALVE`) and an upper working valve (`WH-ZIPPER-VALVE`) with a top elbow and flanged outlet (`WH-ZIPPER-OUTLET`) toward the tree; actuators point away from the trees. The procedural zipper and the CadQuery zipper share these names; the simulator binds well 1 to the store valves `iso`, `zipIso`, and `zipWork`.
- Missile: low-pressure side is two suction headers along the deck edges with an outlet per pump (`PP-MISSILE-LPHEADER`, `PP-MISSILE-LPOUTLET`); high-pressure side is the discharge header of junction fittings down the centerline (`PP-MISSILE-HPHEADER`) with a check valve and swivel-arm stub per feed port (`PP-MISSILE-CHECKVALVE`, `PP-MISSILE-SWIVELARM`), a transducer, the relief valve with its vent line (`PP-MISSILE-PRV`), and the outlet flange (`PP-MISSILE-OUTLET`). Pumps park nose-in on both sides at one pitch per fitting; the simulator lengthens the missile with the pump count computed from the fleet and the rating.
- Sliding sleeve path: the tree carries a ball launcher (`WH-FRACTREE-BALLLAUNCHER`, scene only) in place of the top adapter; the downhole scene draws sleeve subs (`DT-FRACSLEEVE`, `DT-TOESLEEVE`) with port windows, inner sleeve, seat, and ball, and openhole packers (`DT-OPENHOLEPACKER`) when that system is chosen. The cut-away model `DT-FRACSLEEVE.glb` shows the sleeve shifted open with the ball seated and the casing joints as ghost context.
- Pad Setup and basins: terrain is procedural (value noise relief flattened across the pad, vertex-colored ground, instanced vegetation by type and density, sky and fog colors) from the basin table in `src/sim/store.js`. Basin values are typical public ranges rounded for training and loaded as editable starting values; they are not operator or well data.
- Fleet power: `PP-POWERGEN` draws the unit for the fleet chosen (turbine generators and switchgear, reciprocating gensets, grid substation, gas conditioning skid, fuel trailer); electric pumps replace the engine and transmission with a motor and VFD cabinet (`PP-FRACPUMP-MOTOR`).
- Downhole realism: formation drawn as nine layers with bedding planes and a few natural fractures on the section face; perforations as six tunnels at 60 degree phasing with crushed zones and casing holes; fractures as a main plane with two branches and a stress-shadow taper across a stage; plugs with slips, cones, element, and ball; coiled tubing with a motor BHA; openhole systems draw a rugose borehole wall instead of cement.
- Artificial lift: the production tree takes a `lift` prop; `PumpingUnit` (animated crank, beam, bridle, and polished rod), `EspSurface`, `GasLiftSurface`, and `PlungerController` draw beside or on it, and `ProductionString` in the downhole scene draws the tubing tail with the pump, ESP, mandrels, or plunger. CAD: `AL-BEAMUNIT.glb` (15 nodes, mid-upstroke) and `AL-RODPUMP.glb` (cut-away, tubing as ghost). Sand control: `SC-GRAVELPACK.glb` (cut-away; casing, formation, and washpipe as ghosts).
- Well colors: `WELL_COLORS` in `src/sim/parts/surface.jsx` tints valve bodies on each well's tree and its flanged run from the zipper leg, in well order, so the same color reads at the tree and at the leg; partner wells are drawn in a darkened tint.
- Cut-away models: a script that removes half of a model to show the interior removes the -Y half in CadQuery coordinates, because the viewer rotates Z-up to Y-up and the default camera looks from +Z, which is CadQuery -Y; the cut face then faces the camera on open. The viewer's own section plane (S) is for models that are not pre-cut.
- Training events: `EVENTS` in `src/sim/store.js` lists the injectable faults with the phases they apply to; each has a recovery sequence in `nextSteps` and its own state in the `events` slice (misfire armed, valve fault, sand timer). Random mode draws from the events valid for the phase at a low rate per second.
- Production hookup: the `hookup` slice steps rig, tubing, tree, done; the scene draws `BopStack` and `WorkoverRig` (block travels while tubing runs) until the tree step, then the production tree, and the lift equipment only when done. CAD: `RG-BOPSTACK.glb` cut-away (annular, double rams, spool, adapter, bell nipple; tubing head as ghost).
- Scoring: `DEDUCT` in `src/sim/store.js` holds the weights (interlock 2, valve against the sequence 3, overpressure 8, kickout 10, screenout 12); `EVENT_TARGETS` holds the time-to-recover targets per injected event (1 point per 15 s over, max 8). Alarm transitions in the tick deduct only when the operator caused them (an injected relief valve lift or screenout does not); a valve command is "against the sequence" when it moves a valve out of a state the current `nextSteps` list has already checked off. `scoreOf` grades A 90, B 80, C 70, D 60. The score is a training weight over a schematic model and is labeled as such.
- Guided lessons: `src/sim/lessons.js` lists eight lessons, each with `setup` and `pad` overrides applied to the defaults, an optional `prep(get, set)` that puts the job at the starting point (snapping valves and finishing stages), and ordered `steps` with a `done(s)` predicate, a hint, and an optional valve or action for the button. Only the first unfinished step is evaluated each tick and a met step stays met. A lesson that stages a fault (kickout in L3, screenout in L4) lists the code in `exempt` so it costs nothing; the lesson score adds a time penalty of 1 point per 10 percent over `targetSec` (max 20). Results persist in localStorage only (`src/sim/progress.js`).
- Session summary: `src/sim/SessionSummary.jsx` reads the live job or the `lastJob` snapshot taken on reset; `@media print` in `src/index.css` hides everything but `.print-area` on white. The JSON export is a Blob download; there is no server.
- Pad asset detail (Drop 11): `src/sim/parts/primitives.jsx` carries the shared chassis and fittings: `Trailer` (gooseneck end set by `front`, tri-axle duals, fenders, landing gear, deck grating, rub rails, bumper, lights; deck top at `DECK_Y` = 1.18 m), `Axle`, `Wheel` (dual option), `Handrail`, `Ladder`, `Stair`, `Hose` (sagging tube), `UnionNut` (three-lug hammer union, used by `Pipe`), and `Studs` (one instanced mesh per flange). Surface assets in `surface.jsx` build on these; every asset keeps its record-bound group and part names (`PP-FRACPUMP-n-ENGINE`, `-MOTOR`, `-VFD`, `-TRANSMISSION`, `-POWEREND`, `-FLUIDEND`, blender `-TUB`, `-AUGER`, `-DISCHARGEPUMP`, `-SUCTIONPUMP`, and so on) so hover picking and the build check are unchanged. Proportions come from the pad photographs (engine-to-fluid-end split, hood height, tank length to width, silo height to diameter); no livery, logo, or distinctive body shape is reproduced. Painted surfaces use the paint materials (`paintWhite`, `paintRed`, `cream`, `chassis`) rather than bare `steel`, which renders dark without an environment map; the rendering pass is a separate drop.
- Draw-call budget: repeated detail is merged into one BufferGeometry per material with `Merged` (`primitives.jsx`), keeping indexed geometry; a gate valve is five meshes (body, steel parts, stem, handwheel or actuator, lamp) instead of sixteen; a tree or a zipper leg draws every stud in one `StudField` instanced mesh; cylinders take their segment count from their radius (`segFor`). Small parts (studs, rails, ladders, hoses, union nuts, rims) do not cast shadows. Reference at the default 4-well pad: about 2,700 draw calls per frame including the shadow pass and 420k triangles; the Drop 10 pad was 3,450 calls and 340k triangles. `window.__padworksStats` and `window.__padworksScene` expose the counters and the scene for the headless checks (`scripts/probe-perf.mjs`).
- Rendering (Drop 12): `src/sim/parts/lighting.jsx` holds the sky dome (`SkyDome`, vertex-colored sphere from the basin's sky, fog, and ground colors with a zenith blend and a sun disk), the environment (`PadEnvironment`, a drei `Environment` portal that renders the same dome once into a 128 px cube map; keyed on the basin colors so it re-renders on a basin change), the canvas textures (`noiseTexture` tileable fractal value noise, `padTexture` gravel with ruts and stains, `wearTexture` shared roughness variation for paint, `blobTexture` radial contact shadow), and `BlobShadow`. Materials live in `MAT` (`primitives.jsx`): metals carry metalness 0.8 to 0.95 with roughness 0.28 to 0.45; paints are made by `paint(color, roughness, metalness)` and share the wear map as `roughnessMap` (roughness only, so UV scale does not matter); rubber and tires are dielectric and rough; glass is a dark near-mirror. Lights: directional 2.4 warm with soft shadows (bias -0.0004, normal bias 0.03), ambient 0.12, hemisphere 0.25, environment intensity 0.9, exposure 1.05 under R3F's default ACES tone mapping. `LITE` (query `?lite=1`, localStorage `padworks.lite`, or a phone-width screen by default) switches to hard shadows, no environment map, and no bump maps; `setLite` saves the choice and reloads. No image assets: everything is generated on load, and nothing comes from a manufacturer.
- Pad life (Drop 13): `src/sim/parts/life.jsx` holds `Sign` (canvas text plate, optional post, `danger` for the red band layout), `HazardStrip` (tileable yellow and black stripes), `Crew` (five merged meshes, poses stand, point, kneel, idle sway), `Windsock` and `Flag` (vertex ripple), `RovingPickup` (drives the lease road out and back), `ExhaustPlume` (four sprites per stack, rising, spreading, fading), `Gauge`, and `HosePair`. Hydraulic hoses are `Hose` tubes (one draw call each): five pairs per tree plus a trunk, two pairs per zipper leg plus a trunk, so a 16-well pad adds about 300 draw calls; the default pad sits at about 3,100 calls with the shadow pass. Placard wording is generic ("DANGER HIGH PRESSURE", "RED ZONE"); signs carry well and pump numbers only. Crew figures wear no livery. The drei `ContactShadows` pass (a scene re-render every frame) is gone; `BlobShadow` planes stand in.
- Vegetation and horizon (Drop 14): `src/sim/parts/vegetation.jsx` holds the plant recipes (`PLANTS`: parts of kind trunk, branch, cone, blob, tuft with relative sizes and shade offsets), `Vegetation` (one `Instanced` mesh per recipe part plus rocks; instance colors come from `shade(base, k, v)` on the basin's `vegColor` and `ground`), `Horizon` (mesa, ridge, or tree line ring at 330 to 580 m, chosen from the basin's vegetation type and relief), and `RoadFurniture` (fence posts instanced, three wire runs each side, cattle guard, gate signs). `terrain.js` holds the noise and height functions, re-exported from `surface.jsx`. The ground has an outer 2 km coarse plane so the horizon stands on land. Counts: full 1,200 plants (1,700 for pine and hardwood), lite 550; rocks 180 or 80.
- Downhole rendering (Drop 15): `rockTexture(kind)` in `lighting.jsx` makes the sandstone, shale, and limestone grayscale maps (bedding and joints baked into the noise); `LAYERS` in `downhole.jsx` assigns a kind and color per bed, and `Formation` tiles the map ten times along the section with bump in full quality. `fractureAlpha()` is the irregular wing edge, `dotTexture()` the proppant grain sprite. The downhole `Canvas` uses `PadEnvironment` with an underground palette at 0.55 intensity, a warm key light with a 2048 shadow map fitted to the section (bias -0.0003, normal bias 0.02), a cool fill, and exposure 1.1; tool meshes cast shadows. `window.__padworksSim` exposes the store for the headless checks only (no telemetry, nothing is sent).
- Cellar wellheads (Drop 16): `Wellhead` in `surface.jsx` draws the casing head, casing spool with side outlet valves, and tubing head from the cellar floor (`-CELLAR_DEPTH`) up to `WELLHEAD_TOP` (0.5 m above grade); `treeDims`, the production tree, and the BOP stack all build on `WELLHEAD_TOP`. `Cellar` is a real pit: floor and walls draw first (group `renderOrder` -2), then a depth-only mask over the opening (`colorWrite` off, `depthFunc` AlwaysDepth so the depth write happens; a disabled depth test would skip it) so the ground, pad, and containment planes fail the depth test inside the opening, then everything above grade draws normally. Anything that must show inside the pit goes in a `renderOrder={-2}` group; a blob shadow over the opening would paint over it, so the trees have none.
- Pad layout rules (Drop 16): nothing passes through anything. The blender parks behind the missile's low-pressure end (`bZ = rear + 8.2` in `SurfaceScene.jsx`) with its discharge manifolds 3 m from the headers, hydration and chemical units beside it on the pump side, silos and the collecting belt between the silo rows with the incline (60 degrees, 11 m) landing on the blender hoppers; the pad grows with the spread (`z1 = max(rowLen + 50, bZ + 22)`). The accumulator unit (`Accumulator`, record `WH-FRACVALVECONTROL`) sits at (-2, -22), outside the red zone, and takes two ground bundles: the tree trunks along `HOSE_BUNDLE_X` (over the containment berm, then down the row) and the zipper leg trunks along `ZIPPER_BUNDLE_X`, both drawn as `PipeRun` with the hose material. `RedZone` is a rectangle (x -34 to 10, z -14 to rowLen + 10) around everything holding treating pressure, with `RED ZONE` signs at three corners; the fuel gas trailers park on the entrance side (x 12, z -42.5 up), the water tanks at x -80, so no unit overlaps another at any pump count from 4 to 24.
- Accounts (Drop 16): the site is gated by `RequireAuth` (`src/auth/`), which checks `/api/auth/me` once, shows `Login` or the forced `ChangePassword`, and loads `/api/me/progress` into `useSim.hydrateProgress` on sign-in. `src/sim/progress.js` posts lesson results (with the job snapshot as a summary) and free-play job summaries (on reset or back-to-setup when any valve was moved); `SessionSummary` takes a `job` prop to show a saved payload. The API is `server/api.js` behind `api/index.js`: sessions are HS256 JWTs in an httpOnly cookie carrying the user's `token_version`, which every password change, reset, role change, and disable bumps, so old sessions die at the next request; passwords are bcrypt (cost 10); eight failed sign-ins per username in 15 minutes pause that name; mutating requests must be JSON (no cross-site form posts). `server/db.js` runs the schema (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`) on the first request and seeds the first admin from `PADWORKS_ADMIN_USER` and `PADWORKS_ADMIN_PASSWORD` when the users table is empty. Locally `scripts/dev-server.mjs` serves `dist/` and the API on PGlite; the headless pass signs each browser context in through the API (`signIn` in `scripts/shots.mjs`).
- Record viewer (Drop 17): `src/library/Viewer.jsx` rebuilds normals with `toCreasedNormals` (32 degrees) because the CadQuery exports carry no normals and the loader would flat-shade them; `classify(color)` maps the export color to a material class (lightness under 0.12 elastomer; brass hue with saturation above 0.25 bronze; saturation above 0.3 paint; otherwise steel with roughness by lightness, lighter meaning machined) and the environment map does the rest, so the CAD scripts only need to keep coloring parts by function. Section mode uses `onBeforeCompile` to paint back faces (`gl_FrontFacing` false) a solid cut color through a `uCut` uniform per material. `Backdrop` is a vertex-colored sphere, `Ground` a `shadowMaterial` disc at the model's lowest CAD Z (viewer Y after the Z-up rotation), `Turntable` sets `autoRotate` on the controls until the first pointer or wheel event on the canvas, and `PartLabel` places a `Label` sprite at the top of the selected part's bounds. `Framer` only measures meshes with a `userData.key`, so the backdrop and ground never enter the framing box.
- Downhole animation (Drop 18): `PlugSetting({ progress })` in `downhole.jsx` is the plug on the string during pump-down (progress 0) and the set sequence (slips first to 0.6, element 0.3 to 0.8, shear and back-off above 0.9); `WirelineString` draws it until the plug is set, then the stage's `FracPlug` takes over and the string sits 0.5 m heel-ward. `PerfFlash` carries the bloom and six `PHASING` jets; clusters fire toe first (`fired = c >= n - clustersFired` in `Stage`, `firingCluster = n - clustersFired` in the scene) and the store paces the run at 0.5 progress per second with one cluster per sixth. `CoiledTubing` cuttings are 160 instanced flakes on a spiral that settles toward the low side plus 48 sparks with a short life at the mill; `CuttingsBed` is one box from the heel to the toe-most milled plug, scaled by `1 - cleanup`. `FlowbackFlow` seeds a particle at every fired cluster (or open sleeve), runs it out of the fracture then to the heel, and colors it sand or water by `0.45 * (1 - cleanup)` with per-instance colors; `cleanup` is `fb.cumBbl / 200`. `Fracture` takes `settled` (drillout onward) for the closed look. `FluidFlow` takes `targetOverride` and `spray` so pump-down fluid can run to the string tip without spraying. `FollowStage` targets the plug being milled during drillout and the heel during flowback and production. The downhole canvas exposes `window.__padworksDownView(pos, target)` for the headless checks.
- Postprocessing (Drop 19): `src/sim/parts/effects.jsx` holds the `useFx` store (`wanted` from localStorage `padworks.fx`, `auto` set by the guard, `frameMs`), `fxOn(state)` (never in Lite), and `Effects({ ao, bloom, guard })`, which mounts an `EffectComposer` (multisampling 4, half-float buffers) with `N8AO` (half resolution, performance quality) and `Bloom` (mipmap blur). Per-scene settings: pad aoRadius 1.4 and bloom threshold 1.3 (only the sun and HDR emitters bloom), downhole aoRadius 0.6 and threshold 0.95 (fracture glow, flash, sparks), viewer aoRadius 0.35 of the model radius with the guard off. The guard samples 120 frames, skips the first 40, and calls `autoOff` above `FX_LIMIT_MS` (45). The sun disk in the dome is an HDR color (1.8, 1.7, 1.45, not tone mapped) so bloom finds it. The cellar depth mask writes a flat depth over the pit opening, so occlusion treats the opening as ground; nothing inside the pit gets AO, which reads fine. Keep anything that must not glow below luminance 1 after lighting, or raise the scene's threshold.
- Sleeve animation and cutaway (Drop 20): every half-cylinder in `downhole.jsx` uses `thetaStart = PI / 2`, `thetaLength = PI`, which after the group's -90 degree roll about Z keeps the half at world -Z (the back), so the front camera sees the interior; fluid, cuttings, and flowback particles already stay at z <= 0. `SleeveSub` animates its own `shift` toward `open ? 1 : 0` at 2.2 per second in `useFrame` (initialized at the target so loaded jobs do not replay), moving the inner sleeve, the seat, and the seated ball, scaling each port's steel cover plane by `1 - shift`, and blooming a flash with `sin(shift * PI)` while moving; `seating` shows the ball on the seat during the store's `seat` step before `perforated` flips. `ToePressure` is a translucent emissive half-cylinder in the bore colored by `surfacePsi / toeOpenPsi` while the store's `toe` step runs with pumps on. `BallInFlight` rolls and tumbles and carries a thin ring so the roll reads. `FracPlug` and `SleeveSub` take `dissolve` (the store's `ct.milling` on dissolvable systems) to shrink in place; `Dissolving` draws the fines stream only.
- Step focus (Drop 22): `focusOn({ action } | { valve })` in the store writes `ui.focus` (with a counter so repeated requests retrigger); `FocusCamera` in `SurfaceScene` turns it into a view through `focusView` (tree valves by their `treeDims` heights from the -z operator side, wing B from +x, the zipper leg by `zipperDims`, actions by phase preset or a fixed view) and glides camera and `OrbitControls` target over 0.8 s with an ease-in-out, consuming the request inside the frame loop once the controls exist (a phase change can hide and re-show the tree while equipment loads). Error reports: table `reports` (user_id, record_id, page, message, status), `POST me/reports`, `GET admin/reports`, `PATCH admin/reports/:id` (status), `DELETE`. View-as: `viewAs` in sessionStorage (`padworks.viewAs`), `effectiveRole(user, viewAs)` drives the nav, the admin page, and the badge; the server never sees it.
- Pad corrections, second round (Drop 21): `HydraulicStand` and `standLayout(n, center, radius)` in `surface.jsx` place one stand per well on an arc opening toward the wells (radius grows with n, span 63 to 153 degrees); `SurfaceScene` chains supply hoses stand to stand from the accumulator's front corner and runs each stand's two trunks to a fan point at z -8 and then along `HOSE_BUNDLE_X` and `ZIPPER_BUNDLE_X` (offset 0.08 m per stand so the runs do not share one line) to that well's tree trunk and leg junction. Pump iron: the pump's discharge pipe ends at its nose at (1.4 m from the missile centerline, 2.18 m up, 1.2 m to its +z side) and its suction hose at (1.4 m, 0.35 m, 1.2 m to its -z side); the scene's swivel arm and suction hose end at exactly those points. `RedZone` takes six numbers for an L: x0..xm by z0..zm (missile side) and xm..x1 by z0..z1 (wells side); `zm` is the missile rear plus 1.5 m so the blender stays outside. `WaterTanks`: the crown is the upper half of a cylinder (thetaStart PI/2) scaled to 0.3 in y, the walkway at y 3.08 with a landing over the front end, the stair climbing toward -x at z 7.3 clear of the manifold. `vehicles.jsx`: `PickupTruck` (GEO.rbox sheet metal, own wheels with the axle across the truck), `ParkedPickups`, `RoadTruck({ road, speed, height })` where `height` samples `terrainHeight`; `terrainHeight` now also flattens a corridor 5 m either side of the lease road (blend to 19 m). `GEO.rbox` is a `RoundedBoxGeometry` run through `mergeVertices` so it merges with indexed parts.
